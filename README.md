# [shopgoodwill bid sniper](https://shopgoodwillsniper.com/)

ShopGoodwill Sniper is a desktop auction tool for people who want to plan a bid instead of watching a listing's countdown. It queues a maximum bid and submits it near the scheduled close of a ShopGoodwill.com auction.

## bid sniper for shopgoodwill: how it works

Choose an auction, set the most you are willing to pay, and add it to the snipe queue. The app can use a fixed maximum or Auto-Max, and shows queued listings and their status together. It runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

The bid limit remains yours: automated timing does not guarantee a win or override a higher proxy maximum already submitted by another bidder. Review the listing, shipping, and your total spending limit before scheduling a bid.

## snipe goodwill auctions with a clear price model

The published pricing is a 2% success fee when you win, with an optional monthly plan that removes the success fee. No win means no success fee. Check the [official ShopGoodwill Sniper site](https://shopgoodwillsniper.com/) for current pricing, downloads, and requirements.

ShopGoodwill sign-in happens in the app's built-in browser. The product documentation says account authentication and session data stay on the device. The queue can run while you step away, with audio alerts reporting bid and auction results.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does it guarantee an auction win?

No. It submits the maximum you authorized near the auction's scheduled end. A competing bidder's earlier, higher maximum or other auction conditions can still determine the result.

### Can I choose my own limit?

Yes. Use a fixed maximum for direct control, or choose Auto-Max to have the app calculate a bid from the listing's displayed auction values. Review the amount before scheduling.

### Which computers are supported?

The desktop app is listed for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Download links and up-to-date system requirements are on the [product website](https://shopgoodwillsniper.com/).
